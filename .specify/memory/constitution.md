# GGClips Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Authorized Content Only
The project MUST process, transform, or publish stream content only with documented
authorization covering the intended use. A streamer's permission MUST NOT be treated as
permission for game, music, image, or other third-party rights. Unverified rights MUST be
treated as unresolved, and the affected content MUST NOT be published. This protects partners
and the project from unauthorized use.

### II. Human Review Before Publication
Every generated clip MUST receive human review before publication. Reviewers MUST check that
the clip is relevant, accurately represented, appropriately edited, and compliant with rights
and safety constraints. Automated selection or editing MUST NOT publish directly.

### III. Reporting and Takedown
The product MUST provide a clear way to report content and an operational process to assess
reports and remove or restrict content when warranted. Reporting and takedown MUST be available
from the MVP. The process MUST record decisions sufficiently to support consistent handling.

### IV. Mobile-First, Accessible Viewing
The public experience MUST prioritize fast, reliable vertical video viewing on mobile devices.
Core playback, captions, search, and navigation MUST remain usable on supported screen sizes
and with keyboard navigation. Captions MUST be available for published clips.

### V. Small, Verifiable Delivery
Work MUST follow the approved feature specification, plan, and ordered task list. Each change
MUST have a clear purpose and focused verification appropriate to its behavior; shared
interfaces and end-to-end user flows MUST be checked when changed. The team MUST stop before
implementation when the plan has not been validated, as recorded in the project workflow.

## Rights and Safety Constraints

GGClips serves an 18+ audience because the game is PEGI 18. The project MUST NOT collect or
republish stream material without authorization. No Rockstar or GTA 6 brand assets may be used
until their use is verified. Rights questions MUST be resolved against the project's research
and reliable sources; uncertain conclusions MUST remain marked uncertain. Monetization and
automatic social posting MUST NOT be assumed as MVP requirements.

## Development Workflow

The MVP MUST include a clip feed, vertical player, search, categories, streamer profiles,
sign-in, human clip review, and reporting and takedown. French is the initial language. English
support remains subject to confirmation. Personalized recommendations, comments, likes,
following, automatic social posting, and subscriptions are later or undecided capabilities and
require an approved specification before work begins. Reviews MUST verify applicable
constitution requirements before a change is accepted.

## Governance

This constitution governs project decisions and takes precedence over conflicting local
guidance. The team MUST document proposed amendments in the constitution and review their
impact on active specifications, plans, and tasks before adoption. Amendments require project
owner approval. Versioning follows semantic versioning: MAJOR for incompatible governance
changes, MINOR for new principles or materially expanded requirements, and PATCH for
clarifications and non-semantic edits. Every change MUST update the version and amendment date.
Feature reviews MUST check compliance with this constitution; any exception requires explicit
project owner approval recorded with the relevant change.
